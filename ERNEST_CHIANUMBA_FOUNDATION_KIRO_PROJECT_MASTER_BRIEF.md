# 3. Foundation Story

The Foundation exists because its founder has seen people in Nigeria whose lives have been shaped by lack.

The founding story identifies realities such as:

- bright minds being dimmed by poverty;
- illness taking away futures that medical care might have saved;
- young people needing opportunity;
- people being overlooked, underserved, or forgotten.

The central belief is that every person has inherent worth and potential, and that circumstances should not define the ending of a person's life.

The website should communicate this story with dignity.

It should not use manipulative or exploitative charity language.

The visual and written direction should communicate:

- human dignity;
- compassion;
- hope;
- practical action;
- opportunity;
- responsibility;
- long-term support.

---

# 4. Mission

The Foundation's mission is to extend God's love to people who are overlooked, underserved, or forgotten by providing practical care, education, and support that restore dignity and build hope, starting in Nigeria.

The mission should remain faithful to the approved Foundation wording.

The Foundation is faith-inspired but inclusive.

The website should communicate its Christian/faith foundation clearly without presenting the Foundation as exclusive to one group.

---

# 5. Vision

The Foundation's vision is a Nigeria where vulnerable individuals are not abandoned to circumstance but are supported with care, guided with wisdom, and empowered to live meaningful lives.

It also envisions communities where love is expressed through action and people can thrive.

The website should make this vision emotionally compelling without inventing evidence that the vision has already been achieved.

---

# 6. Core Values

The Foundation has five core values.

## 6.1 Love in Action

Love should be expressed through practical action rather than words alone.

## 6.2 Compassion Without Condescension

People should be treated with dignity and respect rather than pity or humiliation.

## 6.3 Stewardship and Integrity

Resources, relationships, and responsibilities should be handled transparently and responsibly.

## 6.4 Faith Rooted, Arms Wide

The Foundation is rooted in faith while remaining welcoming and inclusive.

## 6.5 Sustainability Over Spectacle

The Foundation prioritizes meaningful, sustainable support rather than short-lived displays of charity.

These values should influence both website content and design decisions.

# 8. Abba's Haven

Abba's Haven is a flagship initiative of the Ernest Chianumba Foundation.

It is important that the website does not incorrectly describe Abba's Haven as an existing physical facility.

The approved Foundation concept states that:

**Abba's Haven is not a physical location, but the experience of being held in care, guided with wisdom, and supported with grace.**

Every person served, partnership, and initiative can be part of this experience.

A physical facility may be possible in the future, but the initiative begins programmatically through:

- partnerships;
- sponsored services;
- community support;
- practical care.

### Website positioning

A strong conceptual line for the website is:

**“The Haven is wherever love meets need.”**

This is a design/content direction derived from the approved Abba's Haven concept.

The site should explain that the Haven represents an experience of care rather than implying that a physical building currently exists.

# 13. Financial Stewardship

The Constitution states that Foundation income may come from sources including:

- trustee contributions;
- donations from government or other organisations;
- charitable/international organisations;
- grants from government;
- grants from international organisations;
- grants from individuals.

Foundation funds are to be applied to the Foundation's purposes and necessary operations/professional services.

The Constitution provides for:

- commercial bank accounts;
- independent qualified auditors appointed annually;
- audited financial statements;

The website should use these documented governance provisions to establish trust.

# 55. Stripe

Stripe is the intended donation payment provider.

Before implementation:

1. Confirm the Foundation's Stripe account.
2. Confirm the account country.
3. Confirm payment activation.
4. Confirm supported payment methods.
5. Confirm settlement/payout configuration.
6. Confirm supported currencies.
7. Confirm whether recurring payments are enabled.
8. Configure test mode.
9. Test successful payments.
10. Test failed payments.
11. Test webhook behaviour.
12. Confirm production configuration before launch.

Never hard-code secret Stripe keys into frontend code.

Use environment variables.

Example:

```text
STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=
NEXT_PUBLIC_STRIPE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY=
STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=
```

Actual secret values must never be committed to Git.
